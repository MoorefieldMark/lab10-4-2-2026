## Lab Questions

### What is difference between edge and level sensitive circuits?

Edge sensitive circuits are only able to change with the rising or falling of the clock signal. When it comes to level sensitive circuits its able to change at any point.

### Why is it important to declare initial state?

It ensures that the system starts in a known state. Without it being in a state that we are aware of it produces unknown values, and we can't get the information that we want.

### What do edge sensitive circuits let us build?

They allow us to build registers, counters, along with other things that have to do with memory.
